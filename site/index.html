Pump.fun bundle risk

Check a mint for wallets that bought the launch together.

Curvewatch is a research-only monitor, trade collector, and paper-strategy dashboard for Pump.fun. This page is a risk reading on that tape: same-slot buyers, the supply the tape still credits to them, and a market-cap band for a later coordinated sell.

Risk signal only. A bundle reading is not a guarantee that anyone will sell, and a quiet reading is not a clearance. Unvalidated until it is backtested and tracked prospectively. Not financial advice.

The collector holds no wallet keys and places no trades. This page has no account and no wallet. Stack: Node.js 20, Express, SQLite.

Demo mode. No risk API is configured, so the panel is a fictional example.

01 — Measures

What a reading is allowed to claim

The panel is shaped around the bundle detector that already exists, plus two fields the collector does not compute. The JSON shape is GET /api/risk/:mint, contract risk.v1, documented in site/README.md. That route is not implemented in this repository.

SAME-SLOT BUYS

Implemented

Token.features() in src/engine/token.js sums SOL bought by wallets other than the creator in the create slot and the next slot. If the create slot was never seen, the same sum uses buys within 1.5 seconds of the recorded create time. bundlePct is that SOL divided by all buy volume on the tape. bundleWallets is the count. The scorer hard-rejects when the share is at least 55% and the count is at least 3. A share of at least 25% is a partial-bundle penalty. Those defaults live in config and can be overridden by environment variables, so a live response must echo the thresholds it applied.

SUPPLY STILL CREDITED

Derivable, not yet emitted

The engine keeps a wallet-to-token map from curve trades and divides holder totals by a 1,000,000,000 supply. The published feature vector does not emit the bundle wallets' share. The risk contract asks a future route to sum balances for the wallets inside the bundle window. Transfers off those wallets are invisible, so the share can be understated after a fan-out. After the active window the map is dropped, and the share comes back empty.

SHARED FUNDERS

Not collected

A trade record has a wallet, a slot, a size, and a time. It does not have a funding parent. Coordinated timing, in this collector, is the slot window itself. There is no second cluster model. The shared-funder block in the panel is a reserved place. Rows there are not detections, and demo rows are excluded from the totals.

RUG ZONE

Unvalidated

No code estimates a market cap at which bundle wallets sell. The contract leaves rugZone empty for a real response. Demo mode draws a round dollar band so the layout can be seen, with confidence left unvalidated. A later estimator would need a pre-registered backtest and then a prospective record on launches it had not already seen. Until that exists, a band is not a forecast.

02 — Collector

Curvewatch is the engine behind the check

The lookup reads a research collector. It watches Pump.fun launches, stores the tape, scores the first minutes on the bonding curve, and runs paper books under stated fees, slippage, and delay. It is paper-only. It holds no wallet key and places no order.

01

Launch monitoring

Redundant Solana log subscriptions see creates, trades, and completes, with a slot number on each trade. PumpPortal supplies create and migration streams. Metadata is read for social links. Every launch is stored so a later label can be attached. The bundle window depends on those slot numbers.

02

Scoring

During the first minutes on the curve, a weighted score and a set of hard rejects decide what may alert. Rejects include serial launchers, creator sells, bundled launches, top-10 concentration, wash trading, a late curve, and non-SOL quotes. A coin that fails once stays out of alerts and out of paper entries. The bundled-launch reject is one of those filters. It is not a clock for a later sell.

03

Paper books

Alerts are entered on paper, late, at the curve price, with a fee, extra slippage, and a reaction delay. Parallel books test pre-registered entry and exit rules. Fills are compared with real wallets on the same token. Positions open during a data gap are flagged and left out of the statistics. Nothing is sent to a chain.

04

Kill rule and evidence gate

The built-in labels are collecting (under 50 closed trades), fail (average return at or below zero), marginal (a positive average whose 95% interval includes zero), and pass (the whole interval above zero). A stricter bar used when several books are tested together also asks for a 99% lower bound above zero, both halves of the test positive, and a better average than a matched random control. The project's rule is to keep money out until a pass on that book's own cohort. Changing a fingerprinted rule starts a new test.

03 — Flow

How a launch becomes a row

The operator dashboard and the optional Telegram lookup stay private. This website does not link to a deployment.

  1. 1

    Feeds

    Two Solana logsSubscribe connections on the Pump.fun program, with the slot on every trade. PumpPortal for creates and migrations. Token metadata for social links.

  2. 2

    Engine, in memory

    Per mint: trades, balances reconstructed from those trades, features, a score on a short interval, snapshots from 30 seconds through 15 minutes, paper entries and exits, and outcome labels.

  3. 3

    Bundle window

    Non-creator buys in the create slot and the next slot. That sum, its wallet count, and the buy-volume share are the detector this lookup is built on.

  4. 4

    SQLite

    Tokens, snapshots, alerts, paper trades, creators, and wallets, in WAL mode. Gap-flagged rows stay in the file and stay out of the statistics. Closed paper rows are not rewritten.

  5. 5

    Operator readouts

    A private dashboard, an optional Telegram card for an allowlisted chat, and a health check. They are for whoever runs the collector.

  6. 6

    Risk API, proposed

    GET /api/risk/:mint would return contract risk.v1. It is not implemented here. With the API setting empty, the panel stays in demo mode and shows fictional rows.

04 — Method

Methodology and honesty

The collector's research rules apply to this lookup. A tidy panel does not promote a replay into evidence.

Separate cohorts

Strategy, scoring, and the paper engine are fingerprinted. Each paper trade stores that fingerprint, and the current test uses only the current fingerprint. The pre-21 October 2026 curve cohort (96f45eab) and the later capital-policy cohort (4033fed1) stay separate. They are not pooled.

Prospective validation

A rule is written down before the trades that count. The pre-registered hypothesis lab counts tokens launched after a test is registered. Editing a definition starts that test at zero. A replay of an old tape describes the past. It does not validate the rule. Historical comparisons in the capital-protection note are descriptive, and their intervals are not a pass.

Random control

At each signal, a matched token of similar age and size is paper-traded with the same exits and costs. The stricter kill rule asks the book to beat that control, not merely to print a positive average. Curve-stage entry failed that kind of comparison and is closed.

This page's own limits

Same-slot buy volume is a measured filter. Supply share is a tape reconstruction and misses transfers. Shared funders are absent. The rug zone has no published backtest and no prospective scorecard. Demo numbers are labeled fictional so they cannot be mistaken for either.
