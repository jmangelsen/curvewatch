## Findings

| # | Severity | Area | Finding | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | High | Graduate labels | After every restart the watcher's in-memory price series started empty, so the 6 h labeler (which reads that series) censored or truncated every graduate snapshot that came due in the following 6 hours. Sandbox evidence: 480 censored rows on mints that are still active, 33k of 77k labeled rows censored overall (evictions explain a large part, restarts the rest). | Fixed: `restore()` rebuilds the last 6 h of each series from `grad_snapshots`; `st.last` stays unknown until the next real poll. | 2 | High | Every restart | Each restart loses all tokens younger than 1 h (`finalized = 0`, marked `gap = 1`) and flags every open paper position as a gap. Production today: five deploy restarts marked 2,290 tokens lost and 6,743 of 9,476 tokens (71%) gap-flagged, mostly through the 30–65 s "no trades" window right after start. | Known and already handled honestly by the code (gap rows are excluded from stats). Operational rule: this deploy is the last restart before Monday. |
| 3 | Medium | Curve snapshots | The 900 s checkpoint is almost never captured (23 rows out of ~118k at 600 s; 2 rows in production) because the active window ends at exactly 900 s and the evaluate loop runs every 5 s. Not a data skew, a missing column. | Queued (engine.js is frozen): take due checkpoints before the window test, or allow `age < activeWindowSec + evalIntervalSec`. |

| 5 | Medium | Column naming | `tokens.unique_buyers_1h` counts buyers only within the 15 min active window (the buyer set stops growing after `toLite()`). | Documented; Studio labels it "buyers, first 15 min". Rename queued. |
| 6 | Low | Checkpoint timing | A checkpoint labeled 30 s is taken at 30–35 s (5 s evaluate interval). Consistent across tokens, so comparisons hold; absolute feature values at early checkpoints carry that jitter. | Documented in Studio notes. |
| 7 | Low | Time-to-graduation | Graduation is only observed for tokens still in memory: 1 h horizon for ordinary tokens, 24 h for alerted ones. Time-to-graduation is right-censored at 1 h; 44% of graduates graduate inside 5 min, median 6.1 min, p75 13 min. | Documented. |
| 8 | Low | Trade persistence | Recorded trades (`trades` table) exist only for tokens a book entered or alerted on; the table is a signal-selected sample and must not be used as a population. | 9 | Low | Codex marks | Codex "approx" positions (restored without a live curve mirror) show gross market-cap multiples while the engine marks executable value after fee and slippage; the two can differ by tens of points on a big winner. | Documented in the Codex UI ("approx" badge). Fix queued in `codex.js` (use `curveSell` on the last recorded reserves). |
| 10 | Low | Mobile navigation | The bottom tab bar had eleven fixed-width tabs and no horizontal scroll; Edge and Rejects were unreachable on phones. | Fixed: scrollable, snap-aligned tab bar. |
| 11 | Low | UI | Two class-name collisions (`.sel` and `.st`) made Codex blotter rows render as flex boxes and the Studio panel ignore `hidden`. | Fixed (`is-sel`, `.studio`). |

Checked and found sound: single trade source (`config.tradeSource`), duplicate-trade guard keyed on signature,
wallet, side and amount; pending-trade buffer bounded (50k mints, 200 per mint, 20 s TTL); paper exits event-driven
on every trade plus a 1 s tick; exit fill after the reaction delay at the then-current curve price; graduation closes
positions at curve close; eviction waits for open paper positions; retention archives before deleting; SOL/USD
refresh falls back to the last good value; Studio runs on a worker thread so a 5 s cohort scan cannot stall the trade
loop.

## What the Studio says so far (sandbox, 14 days, SOL-quoted, finalized, gap-free; n = 43,119, 365 graduates)

- At 60 s, 82% of eventual graduates sit in a rejected group. The pass group graduates at 0.49% while "creator sold"
  graduates at 1.09% and "drawdown from peak" at 2.46% (Wilson lower bounds above the pass rate). The reject rules
  are throwing away graduates; whether those were tradeable after cost is a paper-book question, not a label question.
- Graduates separate from the field very early: the graduate median at 30 s is 78.7 SOL versus 28.2 SOL for the
  field, and 63% of graduates are already above the field's 90th percentile at 30 s.
- After graduation, of 697 watched pools with a known 6 h outcome, 4.9% ran 2× or more, 26.7% held above the
  first snapshot, 34.7% faded and 33.7% died.
- The best single separators at 60 s (curve progress, market cap, net SOL, buyers in the last 60 s) are all "price
  already moved" features: they identify graduates and also identify the entries every long book has lost money on.
  That is the same conclusion the paper books reached, now visible in the label data.
